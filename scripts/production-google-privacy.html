Optional Google Analytics

Google Analytics loads only after you enable Google Analytics in Analytics preferences and select Save choices, or select Accept all. You can choose Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity separately when they are available. Decline all keeps both optional services blocked and leaves all service information, call links and text links available. Your choice is stored in this browser for up to nine months. A functional refusal cookie can preserve disabled choices if browser storage fails. A browser that blocks both methods may ask again on your next visit.

If you accept, Google Analytics uses cookies to measure visits and events on this website. We measure page views and taps on call and text links, together with a page path and a general link position. A tap does not confirm that a call connected, a text was sent or a job was booked.

Our events do not include contact details, vehicle details, location lookup text or message contents. Page queries and fragments are removed from the page address sent by our code, and referring addresses are limited to their origin. Avoid including personal information in website addresses. Google also processes technical request information under its own policies and may process information outside Australia.

We do not enable advertising personalisation or Google Signals in our website configuration. Read Google’s privacy policy and how Google uses information from sites.

Changing your analytics choice

Use Analytics preferences in the footer to change your choices. Disabling Google Analytics stops further events from our tracking code and clears the Google Analytics cookies it can access. It does not erase information already processed by Google. Your choices apply to this browser; other browsers and devices have their own choices. An earlier Google Analytics choice does not grant permission for Microsoft Clarity.
