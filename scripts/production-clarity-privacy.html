Optional Microsoft Clarity

Microsoft Clarity provides session replays and heatmaps to help us understand how people use this website. It loads only after you enable Microsoft Clarity in Settings or Analytics preferences and select Save choices, or select Accept all. Choosing Google Analytics alone does not enable Clarity. Reject optional keeps both optional services blocked.

If you accept Clarity, Microsoft processes interactions such as page views, scrolling, clicks, page layout and technical device information. Clarity uses cookies, including _clck and _clsk, to associate activity with a browser and session. Microsoft may process this information outside Australia under its own policies. We do not send visitor names, contact details, message contents or identity tags to Clarity, and we do not enable advertising consent in our Clarity configuration.

Microsoft and Sampson Heavy Diesel Services act as independent data controllers under Clarity’s terms. Those terms allow Microsoft to collect or receive personal information from this website for Microsoft Advertising, improve its products and services and create advertising profiles. Information that is not personal may also be used for research, development and AI model training and evaluation. Our advertising consent setting does not override these broader terms.

Our website applies strict text masking across the page before Clarity loads. Inputs, the suburb and postcode lookup and its results are also masked. Masking reduces what appears in recordings; it does not prevent Microsoft from receiving technical request information. Clarity is blocked on addresses with query strings or fragments and on paths outside our published pages because page addresses can contain personal information.

Use Analytics preferences in the footer to withdraw Clarity permission. We stop capture, clear the Clarity cookies our website can access and reload the page to end the active Clarity runtime. Activity collected before withdrawal may finish processing. Withdrawal does not delete information already received by Microsoft. Your choices are stored in this browser for up to nine months and apply only to this browser. A functional refusal cookie can keep disabled choices off if browser storage fails. If neither method can save your refusal, the website explains the problem and reloads this page with optional analytics disabled. Existing Google Analytics permission does not grant Clarity permission.
