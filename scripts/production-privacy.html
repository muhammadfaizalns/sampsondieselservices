Contacting Clive

Calling or texting from this website opens your device’s phone or messaging service. Information you choose to send, including contact details, vehicle or equipment details, location and photos, is sent to Clive through that service. Share only what is needed for your enquiry.

For questions about information you have provided, contact Clive Sampson on 0474 372 852. This website has no online enquiry form, customer account or payment checkout.

Hosting and performance

Cloudflare provides website hosting, security and performance services. Hosting and telecommunications providers process technical information needed to deliver their services, including network requests. Their own privacy notices also apply.

Cloudflare Web Analytics measures page views, visit sources, device and country information and page performance. Its analytics beacon does not use cookies or browser storage to identify visitors, and does not track people across websites. These measurements can be incomplete when scripts are blocked. Read Cloudflare’s privacy policy and Web Analytics information.

Location lookup and site files

The suburb and postcode lookup runs in your browser using a downloaded Victorian locality dataset. Lookup text is not sent to a lookup provider or included in our analytics events. Fonts and assembly files are served with the website.

Accessing the content

The assembly viewer is optional. Service information and contact details are also available as text. Automatic views change every 3.5 seconds while the viewer is visible. Use Pause to stop the tour and camera motion, Next angle to change view, or choose an assembly. Your device’s reduced motion preference stops automatic movement and slide changes.

You can use a keyboard to reach links, buttons, the assembly selector and the location search. If something on this website is difficult to use, call or text Clive on 0474 372 852.

About this notice

This notice describes the website and its contact links. For information about how your enquiry or job records are handled beyond the website, contact Clive directly. External links open other websites with their own practices.
